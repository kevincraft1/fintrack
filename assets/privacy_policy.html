Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Short Version: FinTrack Pro does NOT collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All your financial information is stored locally on your device only.

1. Introduction

Welcome to FinTrack Pro ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your financial data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data from you.

  • No account registration required
  • No email address collected
  • No phone number collected
  • No financial data transmitted to our servers
  • No browsing history tracked
  • No location data collected

3. Local Data Storage

All data in FinTrack Pro is stored locally on your device using Isar Database, a high-performance local database solution. This includes:

  • Transaction records (income and expenses)
  • Category information
  • Budget settings
  • Financial goals
  • Wallet information
  • Debt records

Important: This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

4. Permissions Used

FinTrack Pro requests the following permissions on your device:

Android Permissions:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC - To enable fingerprint/face unlock security for accessing the app. This permission allows the app to use your device's biometric hardware for authentication.
  • READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below) - Only used when you choose to export or import your financial data as PDF or backup files.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) - For selecting custom images if you choose to add them to your records.

5. Data Security

While all data is stored locally, we recommend:

  • Enable the app lock feature (biometric authentication) to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regularly backup your data using the export feature
  • Keep your device secure with screen lock enabled

6. Third-Party Services

FinTrack Pro uses the following third-party libraries, none of which collect personal data:

  • Isar Database - Local database storage (data stays on device)
  • Flutter - App framework by Google
  • Google Fonts - For typography (fonts are cached locally)
  • PDF Generation Libraries - For creating export files locally

No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in this app.

7. Data Export & Import

You can export your data in the following formats:

  • PDF Reports - Generated locally and saved to your device
  • Data Backup - Export all data for backup purposes

These files are saved to your device's storage and you have full control over them. We do not receive or access these exported files.

8. Children's Privacy

FinTrack Pro does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. However, since no data is collected at all, this policy naturally extends to all users regardless of age.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and the updated date will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, you inherently have complete control over your information. You can:

  • Delete any transaction or data within the app at any time
  • Clear all app data through your device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data permanently
